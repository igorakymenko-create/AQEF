| 0.34 | 2026-10 | Igor Akymenko | Consistency release arising from a staged external audit of v0.33. Result Resolution: a Human Reviewer's or a requalified Oracle's Result resolves an earlier one without modifying it, and Aggregation reads the effective Result (Volume I, Chapter 6); Result gains `id`, `execution`, `clause`, `resolves`. Quality Gates are blocking or advisory; every evaluation yields a defined Gate Result; an override applies to exactly one Gate Result. A Release Decision references a Snapshot of the configuration it was evaluated under and cannot be recorded if that configuration has since changed; defining or changing a Gate is governed under `contract_define`. Full-run invalidation widens the Seeded Control rule rather than lifting it. Contracts and Quality Gates attach at Project, Suite, and Scenario level and compose additively; one Oracle per clause; voting threshold defined; Scenario `prompt` added. Seeded Controls MUST confirm that their planted defect reached the Evidence, by a Validator, a Human Reviewer, or fixed Evidence. Principals: Person and Service Account; automation may carry out a governed decision but not make one. Conformance Statement added (§10); Minimal Engine wording aligned with §10; a Validator Plugin's registration check described as repeatability, not proof of determinism. Schemas and API updated (Appendix A, B, C); Quick Start rebuilt as a valid configuration; examples updated (Appendix H). |
| 0.35 | 2026-10 | Igor Akymenko | Consistency release arising from a staged external audit of v0.34. A missed Seeded Control invalidates Results without modifying them (derived `invalidated_by`; `inconclusive` keeps its single meaning); Result Resolution is a linear chain, and an `oracle_unavailable` Result may be re-assessed by the same Oracle once it is reachable; Result gains `oracle`. A Gate whose aggregate includes a non-`actionable` Result always fails, listing every reason (`reasons`, new `non_actionable_results`); the configuration waiver is removed. A Release Decision requires at least one applicable Gate, and its currency check covers the Environment and inline Contracts, which a Snapshot now keeps in full. Plugin Invocation states the logical shape of a plugin assessment (Volume XVI). Migration Guide aligned with capability-scoped Conformance. Internal working notes no longer referenced from the published text. Contributors: AI assistance entry corrected. Examples updated (Appendix H, K). |
| 0.36 | 2026-10 | Igor Akymenko | Fixes from a regression audit of v0.35. A Validator or plugin that cannot complete its check produces `oracle_unavailable`, never `fail`, and Validation then has not passed; the same Validator may re-assess once it works again (Volume V; Appendix C). A Snapshot keeps every inline Contract of a level, in composition order. `invalidated_by` lists every missed Seeded Control that reaches a Result, which stays invalidated until the Oracle is requalified for all of them. |
| 0.37 | 2026-10 | Igor Akymenko | Gaps found while writing the Conformance Test Specification closed. Datasets have a place in Project and are referenced by name and version. A clause MAY carry an `id`, and only a shared `id` makes two contributed clauses the same clause. A clause states the Quality Dimension its Results count toward, with defaults by Validator type. Result gains `recorded_at`; an Oracle call that fails in transport before any verdict MAY be re-attempted, every attempt recorded. Appendix C adds operations to record a Human Reviewer's verdict and to request re-assessment. A standing release policy gets a shape and a resource (Appendix A §A.14). GateResult and Release Decision gain `id`, and an override names the GateResult it applies to. A Gate does not pass on Results produced under an Environment or Contract other than its Snapshot's (`stale_results`). Any change to a Contract or Quality Gate, through whatever operation, is `contract_define`. Examples updated (Appendix H, K). |
| 0.38 | 2026-10 | Igor Akymenko | Further gaps found while writing the Conformance Test Specification closed. A Judge Result over several assertions says so (`assertions`). A Result is stale only if its own clause changed, matched by `id`; without an `id`, any change to its Contract makes it stale. A Human Reviewer's confirmation of a Seeded Control and an Oracle's requalification are recorded through the API. `panel` counts samples in a stability configuration. `on_timeout: escalate` passes a review to a named Role (`escalate_to`) and never produces a verdict; `on_disagreement: flag` removed. `cli` added to the capability identifiers (§10). Editorial: two sentences in Volume I, Chapter 6, misplaced by v0.37, restored. |

| 0.40 | 2026-10 | Igor Akymenko | A Seeded Control can be a clean control (`kind: clean`): Evidence free of its defect class that the Oracle must pass. A false alarm on it counts as a miss and is reported as such (`false_alarm`, `seeded_control_false_alarm`); a Project SHOULD keep a clean control for every Judge that feeds a Gate. A Judge can be admitted before use, on its agreement with a Reference Set that Human Reviewers labelled, raw and corrected for chance (Volume VI, Admission; Appendix C, `/admissions`); a Quality Gate MAY require admitted Judges (`require_admitted_judges`, `judge_not_admitted`), and every Report shows Judges not admitted. A Human Reviewer who confirms a Seeded Control SHOULD not know which items are controls. Best Practices extended. Examples updated (Appendix H). |
