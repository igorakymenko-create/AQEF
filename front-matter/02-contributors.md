## Acknowledgements

[Roman Hurakov](https://www.linkedin.com/in/relywoo/) — external feedback that shaped
clause-level weighting and aggregate declaration for Judge Results (Volume VII and
Volume VI, v0.27): a reported case in which a faithfulness Judge returned a high score on
a response whose one load-bearing figure was wrong, and the observation that a per-check
statement of what failure costs is what makes a score actionable.

[Victor Ematin](https://www.linkedin.com/in/victor-ematin/) — external feedback that
shaped Seeded Controls (Volume VIII, v0.28): the observation that evaluation tooling can
stay green and silent on a known, deliberately planted defect motivated testing the
Oracle itself, not only the system under test. The phrase "honest
denominators" (Volume I, Chapter 6) is also his, as is the practice
behind generated, exposure-retired Seeded Controls (Volume VIII, v0.32).

[Tatyana Arbouzova](https://www.linkedin.com/in/tatyana-arbouzova-leader/) — a public
discussion of AI agents acting outside their test environments that shaped the Action
Boundary, Unauthorized Action, and Blocked-Path Testing (Volumes III, VIII, IX, v0.33).
